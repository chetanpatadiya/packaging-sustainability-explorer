Keep beginning inventory, purchases, released-product consumption, scrap and closing inventory in the same assessment period.

STEP 02

Set a reduction percentage only against avoidable waste. Necessary process consumption and usable inventory are excluded from estimated savings.

STEP 03

Review opportunities and evidence

Outputs are scenario estimates, not realized savings. Components failing inventory reconciliation are excluded from the quantitative totals.

STEP 04

Methodology and interpretation

An intentionally narrow mass-balance and scenario model, suitable for early opportunity screening.

1. Reconcile first

Opening + Purchases = Applied + Scrap + Closing + Other

“Other” covers documented transfers or adjustments. An unmatched flow excludes that component from aggregate opportunity estimates.

2. Separate sources of waste

Avoidable = max(0, Confirmed scrap − Necessary process use)

Closing stock that is still usable is not confirmed waste. The at-risk quantity is reported separately and is never added to avoided scrap.

3. Model an intervention

Prevented units = Avoidable units × Assumed reduction %

Prevented mass (kg) = Prevented units × grams/unit ÷ 1000

Annual values are normalized by multiplying the assessed-period result by 12 / assessment months.

4. Interpret financial values

Gross avoided material value = Prevented units × Unit material cost

This does not represent net savings or ROI. MOQ, new equipment, labor, validation, yield effects, logistics, and disposal costs are not included.

5. Quality-system gates

Flag changes affecting labels/UDI, sterile barriers, package protection, and validated equipment or software. These are review triggers; an unchecked box is not an approval or assurance of no impact.

6. Evidence quality

Identify the input provenance (measured, documented estimate, or illustrative) and record the source. Scenario percentages are always assumptions until confirmed by an appropriately controlled pilot.

Source framework: C. Patadiya, The Label Nobody Counted (Healthcare Packaging, 2026); Smithers sustainability printing research and Smithers presentation on demand-triggered labels; general material-balance accounting.
